# styles/

Drop `.md` or `.txt` files here and they appear in **MMH3 Style Pack**'s
`style_file` dropdown. The contents are appended verbatim to `extra_rules`.

Files starting with `_` are ignored, which is why this one does not show up.

## What belongs in here

House styles you reuse, and **style-only** cut-downs of anything else.

## What does NOT

Whole vendor skills. MiniMax's H3 skills are agent procedures for their own hub —
two of them say so outright: *"Requires the MiniMax Hub agent (canvas workspace and
MiniMax H3 generation); not portable to generic agent harnesses."*

Around their style guidance sits: numbered steps, user confirmation gates,
prescribed shot counts (15s → 4 shots, 30s → 5–6, 60s → 7–9), fixed time segments
written for a 15-second clip, and a voiceover the model is expected to generate and
then measure against the video length.

Pasted whole into `extra_rules`, that fights a grid-locked window and a pinned
master audio, and tells the writer to wait for a confirmation that will never come.
Cut the look out and leave the procedure behind — the four look-led skills are
already in the node as built-in blocks.

**The general rule, learned the expensive way: a vendor skill encodes a vendor
pipeline.** Check every rule against who generates the audio, who picks the cuts,
and how continuity is carried, before adopting any of it.
