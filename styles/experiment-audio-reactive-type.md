## LET THE TYPE ANSWER THE VOICE

- The words on screen change WITH the vocal: swelling on a held note, snapping
  smaller between phrases, trembling through a rough one, still on a whisper.
- Tie it to the words and times you were given. Name a moment and say what the type
  does there -- not "reacts to the music", but "on the held note at 00:04.100 the
  letters widen and the spacing opens".
- Weight, scale, letter-spacing and blur are the parameters. Say which one moves, and
  by how much.
- Whether the model couples its own audio to on-screen type at all is the question
  here. IF IT DOES NOTHING, the text must still be legible, placed and sized on
  purpose -- a failed reaction should degrade to good static typography, never to
  nothing.
