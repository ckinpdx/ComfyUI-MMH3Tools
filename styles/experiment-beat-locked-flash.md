## HIT THE BAR LINES

- Use the BAR TIMES given for this chunk. Put a one- or two-frame event exactly on
  them, and name which bars you are using.
- Choose the event: full invert, blowout to white, cut to black, a single frame of
  the negative, a solid colour field, one frame of a different shot entirely.
- NOT EVERY BAR. Two or three across the chunk. A flash on every bar is a strobe,
  not an accent, and it stops registering by the third one.
- The shot underneath continues unbroken. The flash interrupts the IMAGE, not the
  action -- she is mid-gesture before it and still mid-gesture after.
