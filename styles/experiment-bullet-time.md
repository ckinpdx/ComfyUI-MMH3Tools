## FREEZE THE MOMENT, WALK THE CAMERA AROUND IT

- One instant is held -- caught mid-air, mid-turn, mid-throw -- and the CAMERA keeps
  moving through the frozen world.
- Say the ARC the camera takes: a quarter turn, a full orbit, rising as it circles,
  passing from behind her to in front.
- EVERYTHING is frozen: particles, hair, fabric, breath, debris. That suspended
  detail is what sells it -- a frozen figure in an empty room reads as a still.
- Say when time RESTARTS and what happens in the first instant after. The release is
  half the effect.
