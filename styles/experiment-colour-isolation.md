## ONE COLOUR SURVIVES

- Everything is monochrome except ONE hue, which stays fully saturated. Name the
  colour and name what carries it -- her coat, a sign, a single practical, blood.
- The isolated colour can MOVE between objects during the shot: leaving her coat,
  appearing in the light behind her, ending up in her eyes.
- The monochrome is not grey mush. Deep blacks, real highlights, full contrast --
  it should look like a print, not a desaturation slider.
- Say WHEN the isolation breaks, if it does. Colour flooding back on a bar line is
  the payoff the whole shot is built for.
