## MANY OF HER, ONE FRAME, ALL DIFFERENT

- Say HOW MANY, and say they are the same person -- not sisters, not a chorus.
- Give each a DIFFERENT action and a different position in depth: one near and
  turning away, one mid-ground still and watching, one far off walking out. Identical
  copies in identical poses read as a tiling artefact.
- They share the light and cast shadows into the same floor.
- Say whether they are aware of each other. Both are interesting; undecided is not.
- NO split, no tiling, no grid. One continuous space with several of her standing in
  it.
