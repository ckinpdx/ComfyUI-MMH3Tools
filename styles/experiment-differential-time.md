## TWO SPEEDS IN ONE FRAME, NO SPLIT

- Name what is SLOW and what is at SPEED, explicitly, in the same space under the
  same light.
- The slow element should be something that reads as slow when it is: falling water,
  debris, hair, fabric, smoke, sparks, dust, glass.
- The fast element is deliberate and human -- she turns, walks through, reaches past,
  at ordinary speed.
- THEY MUST INTERACT. She moves through the slow debris and it does not speed up
  where she touches it. That contact is the whole test; two unrelated things at two
  speeds is just a busy frame.
- No seam, no panel, no inset. One continuous image, two clocks running inside it.
