## AN IMAGE INSIDE ITSELF

- Name the CARRIER: a screen in shot, a mirror, a photograph she is holding, a
  window, a poster on the wall behind her.
- The carrier shows THIS shot -- including the carrier, including the shot inside
  that. Say how many levels are visible. Three is legible; six is a texture.
- Each level differs slightly: a beat later, a beat earlier, one detail changed. That
  difference is what makes it eerie instead of merely graphic.
- The camera may push INTO one level until it becomes the outer level.
- Keep her recognisable at the second level at least. If the recursion is only
  pattern, it has stopped being an image.
