## BLOW IT APART INTO ITS COMPONENTS

The technical-illustration move: an object separates into its parts, each drifting
out along its own axis and holding in the air in assembly order.

- Name the OBJECT and its PARTS. A radio into case, board, speaker, dial, screws. A
  chair into legs, seat, back, dowels. A room into its four walls and floor.
- Parts travel OUTWARD ALONG AXES, keeping their orientation and their spacing even.
  This is a diagram, not a blast -- nothing tumbles.
- THEY HOLD, suspended, for a beat. The hold is what makes it read as an exploded
  view rather than an explosion.
- Say whether it reassembles, and whether the reassembly runs the same order
  backwards.
- Every part stays intact and identifiable. Debris and shattering are the failure
  mode, not the effect.
