## THE FRAME ITSELF MOVES

The edges are not fixed. Treat the aspect as something the video DOES, not something
it happens inside.

- Bars close in to a slot or open out to full width, and the image re-frames as they
  travel.
- The picture can BLEED past its own border for a moment, or sit inset with the
  surround visible around it.
- Say what is OUTSIDE the image when it shrinks: black, a texture, a second image,
  the type, the same scene continuing dimmer.
- TIE THE CHANGE TO A MOMENT -- a word, a bar line, a hit from the timings given. A
  frame that changes shape for no reason reads as an encoding error.
