## STOP THE WORLD, LET HER WALK THROUGH IT

- Everything except her is frozen mid-motion: rain hanging in the air, a splash held
  open, sparks stopped, people caught mid-step, a coat still flared.
- She moves at normal speed through it, and she can INTERACT -- pushing a suspended
  drop aside, stepping around a stopped figure, passing a hand through smoke that
  does not move.
- Say WHAT is frozen and at WHICH INSTANT: "mid-splash", "at the top of the jump",
  "the moment the glass lets go".
- The frozen things must look like arrested motion, not like objects at rest. Water
  in the air, hair off the shoulder, fabric mid-swing.
- One clock is STOPPED here, not slowed. That is the difference from two-speed.
