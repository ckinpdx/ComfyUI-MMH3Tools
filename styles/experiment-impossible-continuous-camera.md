## ONE UNBROKEN MOVE THROUGH A CHANGE OF SCALE

- Pick an APERTURE to pass through: a pupil, a keyhole, an open mouth, a screen, a
  crack in concrete, the O of a word standing in the set.
- State three stages -- where the move starts, what it passes through, where it
  arrives -- and make the arrival a different SCALE, not merely a different place.
- IT IS CONTINUOUS. No cut, no dissolve, no wipe. Say "in one unbroken move", and
  describe the passage itself rather than only its ends.
- Something carries across the threshold: a colour, a shape repeated at the new size,
  a light that was behind her and is now in front. That is what makes the two spaces
  related rather than merely adjacent.
