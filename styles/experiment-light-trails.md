## LET MOTION LEAVE A TRACE

Long exposure: what moves smears, what holds still stays sharp.

- Say WHAT trails -- a hand, a held light, a passing car, her whole body if she is
  the moving thing.
- The trail is made of the thing's own light and colour, and it FOLLOWS ITS ACTUAL
  PATH: an arc for a swung arm, a spiral for a turn, a straight streak for a pass.
- SOMETHING MUST STAY SHARP in the same frame. Without a sharp anchor there is
  nothing to measure the smear against and it reads as motion blur.
- Say how long the trail persists: a short ghost half a beat behind, or a full stroke
  drawn right across the frame.
