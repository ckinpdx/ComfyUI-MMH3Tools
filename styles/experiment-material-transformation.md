## CHANGE WHAT SHE IS MADE OF, MID-SHOT, WITHOUT A CUT

- Name BOTH materials and the order: flesh to wet paper, concrete to smoke, wool to
  circuit board, glass to meat.
- Name the FRONT of the change -- where it begins and how it travels. From the
  fingertips inward. Up the spine. Outward from a point on the sternum.
- The boundary has a look of its own: a crumbling edge, a seam of light, fibres
  lifting, a line of grain dissolving. Describe the front, not just the endpoints.
- THE PERFORMANCE CONTINUES THROUGH IT. She keeps singing and keeps moving; the
  substance changes underneath the motion rather than replacing it. A body that goes
  still while it transforms is a dissolve between two shots.
- One transformation per shot. Two makes it a morph reel.
