## FLIP THE IMAGING MODE

- The picture switches to another way of seeing: x-ray, thermal, infrared,
  night-vision, sonar, ultrasound.
- Say what the new mode REVEALS that the ordinary image did not -- bone inside the
  hand, heat still in an empty chair, something standing behind her that was not
  visible before.
- Each mode brings its own palette and artefacts: thermal blooms and loses texture,
  x-ray is translucent and negative, night vision grains and casts green, sonar
  sweeps.
- It flips ON a moment from the timings given, and either flips back or does not. Say
  which.
