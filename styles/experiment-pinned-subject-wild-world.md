## PIN HER, SPIN THE WORLD

She does not move in the frame. Everything behind her does.

- SHE IS LOCKED: same position, same scale, same place in frame for the whole shot,
  as if the camera were bolted to her chest. Head and shoulders hold.
- THE WORLD MOVES VIOLENTLY: the room rotates around her, the horizon tumbles, a
  street rushes past, the sky wheels overhead.
- Say the AXIS and the SPEED -- rotating about her head, sliding laterally at pace,
  tumbling end over end.
- Her LIGHT can change as the world turns. That shifting light is what proves the
  world is moving rather than the background being a flat plate.
- The test is that she stays sharp and still while the surround does something
  impossible. If she drifts or smears with it, it failed.
