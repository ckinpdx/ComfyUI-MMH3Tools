## LET IT DEGRADE ACROSS THE CHUNK

The picture is a print, and the print is getting worse.

- Name the PROCESS: halftone dots, four-colour misregistration, xerox generation
  loss, fax banding, newsprint bleed, VHS dropout, thermal fade.
- IT ESCALATES. Clean at the start of the chunk, visibly damaged by the end. Say
  roughly where the turn happens, using the timings given.
- Registration drifts as it goes: channels separating by a few pixels, then more.
- The damage is IN the image, not over it. It eats the picture -- edges break up,
  colour misses its shape -- rather than sitting on top as a layer.
