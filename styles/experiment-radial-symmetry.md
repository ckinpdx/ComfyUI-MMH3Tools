## MIRROR THE FRAME

- State the symmetry and where its axis sits: two-fold vertical down the centre,
  four-fold, six-fold radial about a point.
- FACES ARE THE TEST. Say whether the face sits ON the axis, doubling into something
  uncanny, or OFF it, repeating as a ring of her around the centre.
- It can come and go -- symmetric for a phrase, released on the next line. Say when
  it takes hold and when it lets go.
- Keep the motion legible underneath. A gesture that still reads when mirrored, not a
  wash of pattern that could be anything.
