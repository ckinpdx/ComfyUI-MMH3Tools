## THE REFLECTION DOES SOMETHING ELSE

- Name the SURFACE: a mirror, a window at night, still water, a phone screen, chrome.
- The reflection shows her, but WRONG -- a beat behind, facing the other way, older,
  still while she moves, alone while she is not, already gone.
- BOTH MUST BE IN FRAME TOGETHER and the discrepancy legible at a glance. A cutaway
  to the mirror alone throws the effect away.
- The optics stay CORRECT: right place, right perspective, right falloff. The
  wrongness is behavioural, not a mistake in the geometry.
- Say whether she notices.
