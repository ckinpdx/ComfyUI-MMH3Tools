## FROM TINY TO ENORMOUS, NO CUT

- Start with her SMALL in a vast field. Name the space and how little of the frame
  she occupies -- a figure a twentieth of the height, far off, low in frame.
- End on a part of her filling the frame: an eye, a mouth, the corner of a jaw.
- CONTINUOUS. Say the move is unbroken, and give two or three waypoints along the
  way so the model has a path rather than two endpoints and a guess.
- The environment stays continuous through it -- the same light, the same ground, the
  same weather at every scale. That consistency is what sells the distance as real.
