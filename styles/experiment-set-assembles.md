## THE SET BUILDS ITSELF AROUND HER

- She is there FIRST, standing in emptiness or a void, and the room arrives around
  her: walls swinging up, furniture sliding in, a floor spreading out from under her
  feet, a ceiling closing over.
- Say the ORDER of assembly and where the pieces come FROM -- up from below, in from
  the sides, falling from above, unfolding out of one another.
- SHE IS UNAFFECTED. She keeps performing while it builds; she does not react to it
  or wait for it.
- Give it an END STATE and say when it completes. A set that never finishes
  assembling reads as instability rather than as an arrival.
