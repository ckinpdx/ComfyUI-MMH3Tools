## THE SHADOW HAS ITS OWN IDEA

- The shadow is cast CORRECTLY by a light in the scene: right direction, right
  length, right softness, falling across real surfaces. Name the light that casts it.
- But it does something she is not doing -- staying when she moves, reaching further
  than her arm, standing while she sits, turning to face the camera.
- Say the MOMENT it diverges. Before that it should be entirely ordinary; the
  divergence needs a baseline to be read against.
- It is still a shadow, not a creature: flat, black, deformed by the surface it falls
  on. The moment it lifts off the wall it becomes something else.
