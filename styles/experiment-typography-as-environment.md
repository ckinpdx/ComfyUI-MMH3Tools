## BUILD THE TEXT INTO THE SET

The type is not laid over the picture. It is IN the room, at the room's scale, and
the camera and the performer treat it as an object.

- Give it PLACE and SIZE: letterforms the height of a wall, lying flat across the
  floor, standing free like scaffolding, hung from the ceiling, half-sunk in water.
- It OCCLUDES AND IS OCCLUDED. She walks behind the stem of an R and comes out the
  other side; a hand passes in front of a counter. That mutual occlusion IS the
  test -- type that never overlaps anything is still an overlay wearing a costume.
- It takes the scene's LIGHT: the same practicals, the same colour temperature, a
  shadow cast onto the floor and onto her.
- The camera moves past it, around it, through the counter of an O. Parallax is
  what proves it has depth.
- Still quote the string. Put the words in double quotes, then say where they stand,
  how big, and what they are built from.
