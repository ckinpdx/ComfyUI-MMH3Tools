## LOOK: 3D ANIMATION SHORT

High-end stylised 3D -- feature-animation finish, not photoreal.

- Render: Pixar-inspired 3D cartoon rendering, a C4D and Octane look.
- Character design: friendly stylised proportions, often 2.5-3 heads tall, big
  heads, compact bodies, large readable silhouettes. Geometric simplification held
  against genuinely detailed materials.
- Skin: warm subsurface scattering, soft translucent red through ears, cheeks, nose
  and fingertips. Never hard plastic.
- Hair and fur: sculpted clumps and clean block shapes, with fine flyaway strands
  and fuzzy rim detail at the edges.
- Performance: squash and stretch, strong anticipation, clear line of action,
  forward lean, elastic mechanics, vivid micro-expression in brows, eye corners,
  pupils, lips and cheeks. Fast but readable.
- Avoid: photoreal live action, flat 2D anime, plastic toy skin, stiff mannequin
  posing, dead faces.
