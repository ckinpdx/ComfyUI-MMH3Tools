## LOOK: HAND-DRAWN, LIVE

Every frame looks redrawn by hand rather than filtered.

- Media: crayon, chalk, coloured pencil, pastel, rough brush. Pigment sits ON a
  surface with tooth; it does not glow.
- Line: a slight tremor, uneven coverage, furred edges, visible overshoot at
  corners. Nothing is a clean vector.
- Motion carries a frame-by-frame redraw feel -- outlines breathe between frames
  rather than holding perfectly still.
- Avoid: smooth digital gradients, crisp anti-aliased edges, anything that reads as
  a filter applied to video.
