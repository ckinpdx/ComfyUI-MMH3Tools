## LOOK: PAPER COLLAGE

Flat cut shapes assembled in readable layers, photographed rather than rendered.

- Materials: flat bold colour fields, black-and-white photographic cut-outs,
  selective coloured cardstock accents, warm cream keylines, soft paper shadows.
- Edges: subtle fibre grain, slightly irregular torn edges, light deckling, visible
  layered seams. Tactile, but not aged, dirty or distressed.
- Depth: clear foreground / midground / background as SEPARABLE paper groups, strong
  subject hierarchy, controlled negative space. Layered, never a flat busy surface.
- Palette: one controlled palette for the whole piece, chosen deliberately rather
  than defaulting to blues and browns.
- Motion is stop-motion assembly: pieces appear one by one, slide or pop into place,
  bounce lightly, press flat, pause, then lock.
- Avoid: fast spinning, digital panning, zooming, smooth layer drift.
