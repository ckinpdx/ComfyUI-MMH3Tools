## LOOK: PAPERCRAFT STOP-MOTION

Physically built and animated frame by frame -- not illustrated with a paper texture.

- Materials: matte tactile paper, visible fibres, folds, seams, torn or cut edges;
  layered cardboard cutouts with real thickness.
- Light: physical drop shadows BETWEEN layers are what define depth. Lighting should
  reveal fibre and card edge rather than smoothing them.
- Depth: multi-plane foreground / midground / background / far background, with
  foreground occlusion. Macro-miniature photography, slight depth of field, 2.5D
  parallax.
- Motion: stepped movement, tiny pauses, slight rebounds, hinged joints, sliding
  paper mechanisms, pieces visibly settling.
- Avoid: smooth CG, plastic surfaces, flat vector backdrops, static backgrounds,
  melting or liquid morphing, high-speed orbits.
